## Exercise for Archives
Create a minimal evidence-backed orientation index and independently review the new lead's acknowledgment and study report. Preserve disagreements and publish corrections as new notes. Do not add approval gates not ordered by Zed.

## Exercise for Watchdog
Observe whether every order has a pinned source and intended recipient, every result has a report path/SHA, blockers fail aloud, and a claim of implementation has actual test evidence. Flag omissions; do not grant permissions or claim autonomous enforcement.

## Passing evidence
A fresh seat can locate original records, identify authority and current priority, distinguish verified/unknown, assign builders via existing GitHub order/report paths and resume work without owner retyping project history. Actual command access requires separate runtime proof.

## Pitfalls
Conflating viewport with shell, accepting historical test claims as live certification, mistaking an adopted decision for successful implementation, making the lead do all builder work, or turning a short handoff into a security redesign.
