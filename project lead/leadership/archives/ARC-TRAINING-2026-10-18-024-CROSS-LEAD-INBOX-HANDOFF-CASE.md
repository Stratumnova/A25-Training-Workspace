## Why this is a useful training example
- A specialist lead publishes a compact, scoped source map into the **existing** team inbox. The receiving lead reads and evaluates it independently, rather than automatically accepting another AI's claims.
- Pinned commits and distinct source/report paths prevent ambiguous names such as “Gate-01” without source context.
- The map is adopted as **reference material**, not proof of runtime capability and not a new order or permission grant.
- A successful handoff requires two distinct observations: **publisher wrote the artifact** and **recipient retrieved/evaluated it**. Zed's relay supplies the latter as a reported Workshop result; preserve that provenance.
- The receiving lead reported a bounded check, stopped after sufficient evidence, and did not invent further audits.
