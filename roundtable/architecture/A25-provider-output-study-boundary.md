## Study question

Can a local AI model enhance the paid use of other AI platforms while remaining a tool, without learning from their outputs through model-weight updates?

## Intended meaning

When Zed says “train A25” in ordinary project work, interpret the request as building and configuring the A25 tool system—readers, rules, code, interfaces, validation and runtime capabilities—unless he explicitly requests model-weight training. A25 is a personal Stratumnova component that supports, rather than replaces, paid provider AI services.

For this study, GPT, Claude and other provider outputs are reference and working records. A25 may retrieve, search, index, hash, quote, transfer and place them in runtime prompt context, and use them to help create games, software and documents, subject to applicable provider terms. Such runtime use does not by itself update model weights.

Do not use provider outputs as datasets for model-weight changes: fine-tuning, distillation, preference/reward tuning, or training an embedding/classification model. Retrieval indexing with an unchanged embedding model is not model-weight training. Zed-authored materials, deterministic test results and appropriately licensed material are separate potential training inputs, subject to their own terms and permissions.

## Single-location boundary

This file is the sole **active reference** for this provider-output distinction. Do not copy its policy into other A25 modules, prompts, check files, or parallel rulebooks. Do not inject it into routine A25 context when irrelevant. Historical proposals, reviews, and architecture records remain provenance, not additional active policy authorities.

This records the study's operational boundary, not legal clearance or an interpretation of platform contracts. Exceptions involving provider outputs for model-weight training require Zed's explicit decision and a documented check of applicable provider terms.
