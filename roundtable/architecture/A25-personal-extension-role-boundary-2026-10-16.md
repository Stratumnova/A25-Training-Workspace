## Agreed purpose

A25 is a Zed-controlled local component inside the Stratumnova system for Zed's personal use. It extends shared work among subscribed AI services by providing readers, rules, records, deterministic checks, context retrieval, and authorized interfaces. A25 is not intended to replace GPT, Claude, or other provider services, nor to become a general-public chatbot product. Existing provider services remain separate actors under their own subscriptions, terms, and capabilities.

The local model is one component among tools and readers. A25 does not acquire authority over Zed or other AI seats. Roles and technical namespaces should be provider-neutral (for example `seat: lead` or `seat: reviewer`); provider identities belong in attribution values. Shared standards proposed by either AI seat require review by the other before adoption. Permanent lore identities remain Zed's decision; functional training names are allowed, and builders will handle project-specific naming during future official-build migration.

## Provider-output boundary pending formal approval

Claude has proposed a separate rule: provider outputs remain reference records and runtime working information, not weight-training data. This proposal is supported by GPT and Claude but is not recorded here as formally Zed-approved. The independent draft must be preserved and presented for Zed's explicit decision. Runtime retrieval, record movement, and ordinary code/document creation are distinct from changing model weights, and provider terms still apply.

## Status

This architecture statement records Zed's existing direction. It does not assert implementation, completed integrations, legal clearance, or authorization to train on provider outputs.
